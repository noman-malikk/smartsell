Before placing this trade

    Planning estimates only. No trading orders are sent. Gross PnL excludes trading fees, funding and slippage; net losses may exceed your selected gross risk. Verify your exchange's liquidation price and stop trigger settings before trading.

    Understand the setup

    Risk first. Position size next.

    Have a stop in mind?

    Quantity equals maximum loss divided by the distance to your stop. Your take-profit result follows from that quantity. A different profit objective never moves your chosen stop.

    Leave the stop blank

    Quantity comes from desired profit divided by the distance to take profit. The calculator then derives a stop from your risk budget. This is money management, not chart analysis.

    Keep margin separate

    Position value equals quantity × entry price. The calculator finds the lowest integer leverage up to 10x that fits the 80% margin budget. Check account-specific leverage limits on your exchange.
